# Phase-II Western manuscript claim–evidence map v0.1.5

## Purpose and use

This map ties the major claims in `PHASE_II_MANUSCRIPT_SKELETON_v0.1.5.md` to frozen repository evidence. It is a drafting control against claim drift, not a new analysis artifact. Repository paths are relative to the repository root. `Unavailable` means the planned semantic estimate does not exist in this study version; it does not mean a null effect. `Citation-needed-external` identifies general background or methodological claims that require a later literature search and must not be represented by an invented reference.

## Drift-control rules

1. Do not replace `unavailable` with an effect estimate, a zero, or a qualitative answer.
2. Do not infer W-RQ2 or W-RQ3 from Stage-B completion or from unjudged generated text.
3. Do not reinterpret judge qualification artifacts as formal semantic research data or comparative model-quality results.
4. Do not pool the original Stage-B outage epoch with the primary repeat.
5. Preserve the 48-case versus 42-case distinction and do not score the six insufficient cases as primary-gold retrieval failures.
6. Treat artifact paths and hashes as repository evidence, not fabricated external citations.
7. Replace citation placeholders only after a documented literature search and source verification.
