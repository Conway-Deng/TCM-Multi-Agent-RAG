## 4. Key Limitations

- **Pilot corpus only:** 16 systematic reviews across 4 symptom domains (271 chunks).
- **48-case benchmark:** AI-assisted drafting and secondary review on pilot topics.
- **Fixed generator:** Single local/hosted generator (`Qwen/Qwen3-8B`).
- **Single GPT-5.6 Sol evaluator:** All semantic annotations generated by one AI evaluator.
- **No human adjudication:** No human graders participated in scoring.
- **No clinician adjudication:** No medical doctors or clinical experts evaluated answers.
- **No inter-rater reliability:** Multi-judge agreement was not estimated.
- **No judge-family robustness:** Alternative evaluator model families were not evaluated.
- **Evidence-grounded evaluation is not clinical validation:** Measures textual grounding and passage alignment, not clinical correctness or patient safety.
- **No causal inference:** Observational descriptive differences between retrieval conditions and semantic outcomes; no causal inference between retrieval and semantic outcomes.
