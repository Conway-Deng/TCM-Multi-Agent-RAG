# Phase-II Western manuscript claim–evidence map v0.1.5

## Purpose and use

This map ties the major claims in `PHASE_II_MANUSCRIPT_SKELETON_v0.1.5.md` to frozen repository evidence from Western Formal Study v0.1.5 and the separate Western Semantic Follow-up v0.2. It is a drafting control against claim drift, not a new analysis artifact. Repository paths are relative to the repository root. `Unavailable within v0.1.5` means the planned automated Stage-C estimate does not exist in that historical study version; it does not mean a null effect and does not negate the separate follow-up estimates. `Citation-needed-external` identifies general background or methodological claims that require a later literature search and must not be represented by an invented reference.

## Drift-control rules

1. Do not replace `unavailable within Western Formal Study v0.1.5` with an effect estimate, a zero, or a qualitative answer; keep Follow-up v0.2 estimates explicitly separate.
2. Do not infer W-RQ2 or W-RQ3 from Stage-B completion or from unjudged generated text; use only the frozen follow-up results for post-study semantic estimates.
3. Do not reinterpret judge qualification artifacts as formal semantic research data or comparative model-quality results.
4. Do not pool the original Stage-B outage epoch with the primary repeat.
5. Preserve the 48-case versus 42-case distinction and do not score the six insufficient cases as primary-gold retrieval failures.
6. Treat artifact paths and hashes as repository evidence, not fabricated external citations.
7. Replace citation placeholders only after a documented literature search and source verification.
8. Do not treat a single GPT-5.6 Sol judgment set as human, clinician, or multi-judge validation.
9. Do not infer causality from descriptive alignment between Stage-A retrieval and follow-up semantic outcomes.
