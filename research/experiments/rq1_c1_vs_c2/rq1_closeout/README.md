# RQ1 C1-vs-C2 closeout

Status: **RQ1 COMPLETE — CONFIRMATORY EXTENSION COMPLETE**.

This directory preserves the original three-repeat controlled study (Study 1A) and adds the independent 100-question confirmatory study (Study 1B). Raw results remain referenced in place and are not duplicated here.

Final conclusion: Study 1A showed a small favorable C2 Full Recall direction that was not statistically established. That direction did not replicate in Study 1B, where C2 was essentially tied with and slightly below C1. The confirmatory CI rules out a >=5-percentage-point C2 benefit under this setup without constituting a formal equivalence claim. RQ1 is permanently closed for the current research scope.
