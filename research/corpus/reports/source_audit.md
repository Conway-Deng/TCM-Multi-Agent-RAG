## Gate decision

- No substantive third-party raw dataset will be committed to Git.
- SymMap and TCMBank may be downloaded into the gitignored local raw area for this non-commercial research build, with source hashes and access dates recorded.
- Generated normalized records and chunks from restricted sources remain local-only pending human confirmation of redistribution rights.
- TCMM code is reference material only; no unlicensed code or LLM-generated facts will be copied.
- CPMCP remains a precise manual-import path until the official files and terms can be verified without scraping or bypassing controls.
- ZhongJing resources remain isolated evaluation candidates; their answers are never corpus inputs.

## Human follow-up required

1. Ask SymMap and TCMBank maintainers to confirm permission to redistribute normalized factual records/chunks in a public research repository and production demo.
2. Ask CPMCP maintainers for the current official bulk-download path and database reuse terms.
3. Ask the ZhongJing maintainers to add an actual license file and document upstream article/image rights and expert-review evidence.
