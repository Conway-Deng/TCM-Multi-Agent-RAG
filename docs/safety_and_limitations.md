# Safety and limitations

Routing supports `supported`, `insufficient_information`, `out_of_scope`, `safety_critical`, and `evidence_insufficient`. Safety-critical requests abstain before retrieval/generation. Agents can abstain when evidence is unavailable.

The system does not intentionally generate individualized herbal dosing/prescribing or acupuncture procedures. It is educational research software, not diagnosis, treatment, triage, or qualified care.

Tests do not prove clinical safety. LLM confidence is not correctness. TCM traditions are not automatically scientifically validated. Source categories are not equivalent. Judges are not ground truth. RQ3 and RQ6 limitations must remain visible.
