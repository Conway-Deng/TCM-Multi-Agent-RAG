# Limitations

- This is a research prototype, not a diagnosis or prescription system.
- The TCM corpus is limited in scope and intentionally conservative.
- All current knowledge entries require human review.
- Formula names are educational examples only; no dose, preparation, or treatment instruction is provided.
- Retrieval thresholds are experimental.
- Semantic retrieval and reranking are implemented as explicit optional hooks, not silently enabled.
- The frontend is a standalone static demo and not a production medical product.
- Automated tests evaluate routing, grounding structure, and safety behavior; they do not clinically validate TCM knowledge.
